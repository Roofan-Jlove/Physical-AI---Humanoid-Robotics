## Citation Format

When citing sources, use APA format:

**Book**:
Author, A. (Year). Title of book. Publisher.

**Journal Article**:
Author, A., & Author, B. (Year). Title of article. Journal Name, volume(issue), pages.

**Website**:
Organization. (Year, Month). Title. Retrieved Month DD, YYYY, from URL
